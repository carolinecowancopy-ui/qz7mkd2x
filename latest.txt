Blood pressure (month total)
Across all 20 readings taken in July (weeks of the 13th and 20th only), the average was about 113/66 with a pulse near 72. That average sits comfortably within the normal range, well clear of the 120/80 boundary on both numbers. The trend across the month was downward: the week of the 13th averaged ~128/73 — solidly in the elevated range and pulled up by one morning session on the 18th that reached 137/136 systolic, which is into Stage 1 territory — while the following week settled to ~108/64, comfortably normal. No readings were logged in the first or last week, so the picture rests on two weeks of data.

What's working
Calories are steady and on target, and the back half of the month shows the log becoming a daily habit with fuller entries. Protein is well anchored on the days it lands — protein powder, cottage cheese, seitan and Greek yoghurt do the heavy lifting — and the week of the 27th cleared 100g on 4 of 5 days. The single high BP morning on the 18th resolved to comfortably normal readings the following week.

What needs improvement
Protein is the recurring gap: it missed 100g on half the logged days, and the monthly average slipped just under the floor — the number that matters most for muscle and bone preservation through perimenopause. Both exercise targets came in under for the month (5/8 sprints, 7/12 lifts), partly because the first week is barely logged. BP coverage is thin — only two of four weeks have any readings.

Suggestions
Add one deliberate protein anchor (a scoop of powder, extra cottage cheese, edamame, or a larger seitan portion) to a main meal on the lighter days to lift the daily-100g hit rate from half to most days. Aim to bank the third weekly lifting session and keep both sprints, which would bring the monthly totals close to target. Take a BP reading roughly weekly rather than in clusters, so each week of the month has at least one session — worth doing given perimenopause is a documented window where BP tends to drift up; the single Stage-1 morning reading on the 18th is worth mentioning to a doctor if morning readings keep landing there, though it stood alone this month.
